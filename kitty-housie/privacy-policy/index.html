We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed in this policy (such as Google) so the App can work, or where required by law.

Game data stored on your device is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Play Games data is managed through your Google account, and you can delete it from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. To ask us to delete any email correspondence or other information we hold about you, email us at the address below and we will do so within 30 days.
